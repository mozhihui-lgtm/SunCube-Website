Our project pathway

From first conversation to an operating SunCube system.

Each farm is different, so the exact scope is defined after discovery. This framework shows how we typically reduce uncertainty, validate the opportunity, and coordinate delivery.

01

Discover

Understand the farm

We review crop requirements, cultivation method, existing infrastructure, site conditions, operational priorities and the problem the project needs to solve.

  • Introductory project call
  • Farm and crop information
  • Initial site material review

OutcomeShared project brief

02

Validate

Assess feasibility

SunCube studies the solar resource, crop-light relationship, climate exposure, physical constraints and potential system configurations.

  • Site and climate assessment
  • Crop-light-energy modelling
  • Early commercial assumptions

OutcomeFeasibility direction

03

Design

Define the SunCube system

We develop the agrivoltaic concept around the crop—balancing useful shade, solar generation, structure, irrigation, sensing and farm access.

  • PV layout and density
  • Structure and farm integration
  • Performance and monitoring plan

OutcomeSystem design package

04

Integrate

Coordinate project delivery

SunCube works with trusted solar, greenhouse and agricultural infrastructure partners to translate the design into an executable project.

  • Partner and component coordination
  • Implementation planning
  • Installation and commissioning support

OutcomeInstalled system

05

Learn & optimize

Measure what matters

After deployment, monitoring can connect microclimate, crop and energy performance—creating evidence for operational improvement and future expansion.

  • System performance review
  • Farm operating insights
  • Optimization and scale roadmap

OutcomeLong-term intelligence

Helpful before we talk

A few details help us understand your opportunity faster.

  • Farm location and approximate area
  • Crop types and growing system
  • Existing greenhouse or shade infrastructure
  • Electricity use or clean-energy objective
  • Photos, drawings or site plans if available
  • Target timeline and decision stage
